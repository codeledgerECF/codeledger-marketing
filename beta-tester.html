Test CodeLedger on camera.

How it works

Four steps. No script, no rehearsal.

1

You book a session

Apply below. If it looks like a fit, we reply by email and pick a time — 30-45 minutes over a video call.

2

You test CodeLedger, on camera

You try it against your own repo, or clone any public repo with 5,000+ files — your choice, or one of ours below — and talk through what you see, in real time. No script — we want your honest reactions.

3

We pay you for your time

You get paid for the session, regardless of what you find. We'll agree on the amount by email before you commit to anything.

4

The recording goes public

We edit the session into a video and post it on YouTube. Clips — with your comments — get shared on LinkedIn, X, Facebook, and Instagram.

Which repo should you test on?

Whatever's real to you. Bring your own repo if you'd rather test on code you know — that's often the most honest test. If you don't have one in mind, clone any public repo with 5,000 or more files, or grab one of these:

microsoft/vscode16,900+ filesvercel/next.js30,800+ filesfacebook/react7,200+ filesprisma/prisma6,000+ files

These are the same public repos we use in our own benchmarks — full clone, no setup beyond git clone.

Who we're looking for

No formal experience required — just real, working code and a willingness to say what you think.

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓
